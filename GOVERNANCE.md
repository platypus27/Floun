# Governance

Floun is an open-source Kryv Labs project maintained in public.

## Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for project direction, issue triage, reviews, releases, security response, moderation, and stewardship of the Floun name and brand. The current repository owner, `@platypus27`, is the initial maintainer.

## Decision making

Routine changes are decided through issues and pull-request review. Maintainers seek evidence, clear product behavior, simplicity, robustness, security, accessibility, and long-term maintainability.

Material changes to permissions, data handling, external services, storage, cryptography classifications, release architecture, or governance require a public design note or issue before implementation. Security-sensitive details may be discussed privately until coordinated disclosure is safe.

If consensus cannot be reached, maintainers make the final decision and document the rationale. Trademark and release-signing decisions remain with Kryv Labs.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer access may be offered to contributors who demonstrate sustained high-quality contributions, sound judgment, respectful collaboration, reliable review, and commitment to the project security and privacy model. Access is granted gradually and may be removed for inactivity, compromised accounts, or violations of project policies.

## Releases

Maintainers follow semantic versioning, publish release notes and checksums, and require the repository release gates to pass. Only maintainers may publish official Floun builds or represent a modified build as an official Kryv Labs release.
